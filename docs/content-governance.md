# Content governance

## Case-study gate

Before publishing a project case:

- [ ] Problem statement approved by the owner of the work.
- [ ] Personal role is precise: principal end-to-end author, with collaborators named when material.
- [ ] No credentials, secrets, internal URLs, client identifiers, or production data.
- [ ] Screenshots and logos have written permission or are replaced with original diagrams.
- [ ] Metrics are sourced, dated, and approved; otherwise use qualitative evidence.
- [ ] English and Spanish versions say the same thing.
- [ ] Links do not expose private repositories or dashboards.

## `ponytail:` ceiling

`ponytail:` local typed content is the v1 ceiling. Move to a headless CMS only when non-developer editing or frequent publishing becomes a real requirement.
